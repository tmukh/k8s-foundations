# Learning log

Record one entry per lab or meaningful session.

## Entry template

### Date — milestone / topic

- **Prediction:** What I expect to happen and why.
- **Command or change:** What I ran or changed.
- **Result:** What actually happened.
- **Diagnosis:** The evidence that identified the cause.
- **Fix:** The smallest correction.
- **Explain-back:** My explanation in my own words.
- **Blog candidate:** What could become a public write-up.
